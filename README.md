# Niche strategy: where to take the book-a-call + email offer next

## TL;DR

**Go after property investing education businesses.** These are coaches and mentors who sell £3k–£30k
($3k–$30k) property programmes through paid ads, a lead magnet, and a booked sales call. Start in the UK, then
move to US land, short-term rental and creative-finance coaches.

Do **not** lead with "income-skill" coaches (setting/closing, AI agency, ecom), even though the current
worksheet targets them. Reasons are below.

The first offer is a low-risk **"Unbooked List Reactivation" pilot**: email the leads who opted in but never
booked, and get paid on the calls or cash it produces. When it works, upsell the full system (calendar,
application, show-up sequence, pre-call emails, deliverability).

---

## 1. Why property education beats income-skill coaches for you

| Factor | Property investing educators | Income-skill coaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Your proof** | $4.6M+ over 4 years for a UK property educator. A prospect hears "I did this in *your* market". | A different market. You'd be translating the proof. |
| **Lead quality** (your main worry) | A buyer needs capital (deposit, refurb money, credit). Qualifying on money is natural and accepted, so the calendar filter does real work. | "Make money online" leads are often broke and young. The same filter cuts volume hard, and the leftover leads still can't pay. |
| **Email engagement** | Leads are 30–60, homeowners and professionals who read long emails. Email is where you win. | Leads are 18–28 and live in IG DMs. Email matters less. |
| **Sales cycle** | Long (weeks to months). Leads need nurturing before they book. **That is exactly the job email does.** | Short and impulsive, driven more by DMs and setters. |
| **Competition for your service** | Few specialists. Most UK property educators use a VA, the GHL defaults, or one Calendly reminder. | Saturated. Cole Gordon / Closers.io alumni and "show-rate agencies" pitch them daily. |
| **Number of buyers** | Dozens of founder-led programmes in the UK alone, and hundreds in the US across land, STR, wholesaling, creative finance and multifamily. | Many, but they churn fast and the space carries FTC/ASA risk. |

Bottom line: your edge is "**I've already done this in your exact market, with your exact lead**". That only
holds in property education. Everywhere else you are one more email agency.

### Sub-niches, in order of attack

1. **UK property mentoring** (BTL, HMO, rent-to-rent, serviced accommodation, deal sourcing, development).
   This is Richard's market, so your proof transfers 1:1.
2. **US land investing coaches.** The model is very similar: ads → free training → "strategy call" → $5k–$15k programme.
3. **US short-term rental (Airbnb) investing coaches.** Same buyer profile (has capital, wants passive income).
4. **US creative finance / subject-to / wholesaling coaches.** Big and hungry, but leads skew lower income,
   so qualify harder.
5. **Multifamily / syndication mentors.** Highest ticket ($25k+), but fewer buyers and longer cycles.
6. *Expansion lane later:* **business acquisition coaches** ("buy a business"). Same "you need capital" buyer, same funnel.

### Before you pitch UK property coaches

You still work with (or recently worked with) Richard Norris. **Check that you have no non-compete or
confidentiality clause** stopping you from working with his direct competitors. Then ask Richard for:
- a written or video testimonial with numbers you're allowed to quote,
- permission to name him and Property Consulting in case studies,
- intros to peers (UK property educators often know each other through events and masterminds).

If you can't name him or can't work with UK competitors, start with the US sub-niches. The proof still reads
as "a property education business".

## 2. Ideal client profile (use this to qualify every prospect)

**Must have all of these:**
- Sells a **property investing programme at £3k / $3k or more** (mentorship, group coaching, done-with-you).
- Sells **on a booked call** (application, calendar, strategy/discovery call). It shows on their site or ad funnel.
- Is **running paid ads now** (check the Meta Ad Library: facebook.com/ads/library). Ads mean a growing list,
  which means a growing unbooked list for you to monetise.
- Is **founder-led**, with roughly 2–25 staff and at least one setter or closer. The founder is the face, and
  their content is the material your emails are built from.

**Strong signals:**
- A "go." / "join." / "apply." subdomain, or a GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels or Kajabi funnel.
- A free training, VSL or webinar as the lead magnet.
- Trustpilot reviews mentioning "saw their ad" or "after my call with…".
- Emails that are weekly-newsletter only, or one Calendly reminder before the call (opt in and see).

**Disqualify:**
- Giant brands with in-house marketing teams (Samuel Leeds, Progressive Property, Pace Morby / SubTo,
  Grant Cardone). These are long-shot pitches, not first clients.
- Low-ticket only (£500 courses, £97 memberships, Skool-only).
- No ads and no list.
- A trust problem: patterns of refund complaints or regulator warnings. Your name ends up attached to their emails.

## 3. The offer

### Front door: "Unbooked List Reactivation" (2–4 week pilot)
- **Problem:** they have thousands of leads who opted in, watched part of the training, and never booked.
  Those leads were already paid for.
- **What you do:** segment the list (engaged vs dormant; capital level from form answers), clean it for
  deliverability, and write a 7–10 email "book your call" campaign that shows real student stories and states
  plainly who the call is for.
- **How you're paid** (pick one):
  - fixed pilot fee (around £1.5k–£3k), or
  - a small fee plus a % of cash collected from calls booked by the campaign, or
  - pure performance per *qualified, held* call (only if you trust their CRM tracking).
- **Why it works as a door-opener:** no change to their funnel, no risk to their ad account, results inside
  30 days, and the money comes from leads they had written off.

### Core retainer: "Booked-Call Conversion System"
After the pilot, you take over everything between the ad and the call:
1. **Booking page + application:** questions that filter on capital, timeline and commitment without killing volume.
2. **Show-up sequence:** confirmation, a "what happens on the call" video or email, peer stories, and SMS/email
   reminders timed to the booking lead time.
3. **Pre-call indoctrination:** emails that answer questions instead of handling objections, so the lead
   arrives already sold.
4. **Welcome / nurture sequence** for new opt-ins who don't book straight away.
5. **Weekly broadcasts** to the whole list that book calls every week without extra ad spend.
6. **Deliverability:** domain authentication, warm-up, list hygiene, Primary inbox placement.
7. **Monthly report:** booked → held → closed, cost per held call, and email-attributed cash.

Indicative pricing: £3k–£6k/month retainer, or a lower base plus a % of email-attributed revenue.
Adjust after your first 2–3 clients.

### Proof line (use everywhere)
> "For four years I ran email and the book-a-call funnel for a UK property education company. We did $4.6M+
> through ads and email: qualified calendars, high show-up rates, and a list that booked calls every week."

Get Richard's sign-off before you name him.

## 4. Finding and qualifying prospects

`prospects.csv` holds **50+ named property and adjacent education businesses**, found through search, with
priority tiers. These are *candidates*, not confirmed fits. The web pages and Meta Ad Library could not be
opened from this research environment, so **every row needs the 5-minute check below** before you contact it.

**5-minute check per prospect:**
1. Meta Ad Library: search the brand or founder name. Are ads active now? How many? (No ads → drop to tier C.)
2. Website: is there a "book a call", "apply" or "discovery call" button? What's the price, if listed?
3. Opt in to their lead magnet with a dedicated inbox (do **not** book a call; don't waste their closer's time).
   Record what emails arrive over 7 days: welcome sequence? reminders? Primary or Promotions?
4. Find the founder on LinkedIn / Instagram, and the ops or marketing person if there is one.
5. Score 1–5 and fill the `status` / `notes` columns.

**Volume target:** check 10 a day, reach out to 5 a day, and aim for 3–5 conversations a week. 50 checked
prospects should give 15–20 real fits.

## 5. Outreach

See `outreach-scripts.md`. The core move is a **personalised teardown of their own funnel**. After opting in,
send a short Loom or written note:
- what their booking page asks, and what it should ask to filter on capital,
- what happened in your inbox after opting in (e.g. "2 emails in 7 days, both in Promotions, nothing
  telling me why I should book"),
- one specific fix, plus the reactivation pilot as the next step.

Channels, in order: founder Instagram DM / LinkedIn (warm with a comment first) → email to the founder → a
UK property event or mastermind in person (property investor networking events run monthly in most cities).

## 7. Updated target-market worksheet (property educators)

**Market:** founder-led property investing educators (UK first, then US land/STR/creative finance). They sell
£3k–£30k mentorships through paid ads → free training → booked call. Usually 2–25 people, with 1–3 closers.

**What have they tried before?** Raising ad spend for more calls. Hiring more closers. A VA or "email
agency" that sends an occasional newsletter. Calendly's default reminder. Application forms that either let
everyone through or scare everyone off.

**What do they want?** A calendar full of people with the deposit or capital to act, who show up and arrive
already trusting the founder, so closes are predictable and ads can scale.

**Obstacles:** leads who love property but have no capital ("I'll start once I save up"). No-shows on cold
traffic. Rising cost per booked call. Thousands of opt-ins who never booked and get no follow-up. Emails in
Promotions or spam. Long consideration cycles with no nurture.

**Ideal situation in a year:** the same ad spend, more closed students, a list that books calls every week
on its own, and a small sales team that isn't burnt out on bad calls.

**Fear if it's never fixed:** ad costs climb until the funnel stops being profitable. Closers quit. Revenue
swings month to month with ad spend. Competitors with better follow-up take the same leads.

**How it feels:** paying for every call twice. Watching the calendar fill up, then half the calls no-show
or say "I don't have the deposit yet".

**Unique mechanism:** I fix what happens between the ad and the call. That means a booking page and
application that filter on capital and commitment, a show-up system on the booking page, pre-call emails
that answer questions and share student stories, a nurture sequence for long-cycle leads, and broadcasts that
book calls from the existing list. All of it runs on a domain that lands in Primary.

**Proof:** 4 years running email and the book-a-call funnel for a UK property education company, $4.6M+
through ads and email.

**Status quo to attack:** "Spend more, hire another closer", a single Calendly reminder, newsletter-only
email, and nobody watching deliverability.

**Who markets to this audience** (study their ads and content): Samuel Leeds, Rob Moore / Progressive
Property, Simon Zutshi (Property Investors Network) in the UK; Pace Morby, Mark Podolsky (The Land Geek),
Rod Khleif, BiggerPockets in the US.
