# Outreach scripts: property education founders

Rules:
- Opt in to their funnel **before** writing. Every message references something real from *their* funnel.
- Never book a fake sales call. It wastes their closer's time and burns the relationship before it starts.
- Keep the first touch short. The teardown goes in the second message, or in a Loom link.
- Fill in `[brackets]`. Only name Richard / Property Consulting once you have his OK.

---

## 1. First DM (Instagram / LinkedIn), after commenting on 1–2 of their posts

> Hey [Name], I opted into your [free training / masterclass] last week. Quick one: do you know how many
> people watch it and never book a call?
>
> I spent the last 4 years running email and the book-a-call funnel for a UK property education company
> ($4.6M+ through ads + email). I noticed 2 things in your funnel that are probably costing you booked calls.
> Happy to send a 3-min video if useful?

## 2. When they say yes: the teardown (Loom, 3–4 minutes)

Structure:
1. **What I saw** (30s): "I opted in on [date]. Here's my inbox: [X] emails in 7 days, [Y] in Promotions."
2. **Leak #1, the booking page** (60s): "Your form asks [name/email/phone]. Nothing filters on capital or
   timeline, so your closers take calls with people who can't fund a deposit. Here are the 2 questions I'd add."
3. **Leak #2, after booking / after opt-in** (60s): "There's [one reminder / no reminder / no reason to show
   up]. Here's what the show-up sequence looked like on the funnel I ran."
4. **The pilot** (30s): "You've got [estimate: thousands of] opt-ins who never booked. I'd run a 2–3 week
   reactivation campaign to that list. [Pricing option]. If it doesn't book calls, you've lost nothing."
5. **CTA:** "Worth a 20-minute chat this week?"

## 3. Cold email (when there's no DM route)

**Subject:** your [masterclass name] follow-up

> Hi [Name],
>
> I went through your [free training] as a lead. In the 7 days after opting in I got [X] emails. None of
> them told me who the call is for or what happens on it, and [Y] landed in Promotions.
>
> For the past 4 years I ran email and the book-a-call funnel for a UK property education company: welcome
> sequence, show-up sequence, list reactivation, deliverability. It did $4.6M+ through ads + email.
>
> I'd like to run a short reactivation campaign to the people on your list who never booked, [on a
> performance basis / for a fixed pilot fee].
>
> Worth a quick call? I can also just send a 3-minute video of what I'd change.
>
> [Your name]

## 4. Follow-ups (only if no reply)

- **Day 3:** "Bumping this. Here's the video anyway: [Loom]. Two quick fixes you can make today even if we
  never speak."
- **Day 8:** "Last one from me. If list reactivation isn't a priority, who handles email on your side? Happy
  to send it to them."

## 5. Discovery call questions (qualify them like you'd qualify a lead)

1. What's the programme price, and what share of revenue comes from booked calls?
2. Monthly ad spend? Cost per booked call? Show rate? Close rate?
3. How big is the list, and how many opt-ins never booked in the last 6–12 months?
4. What happens today after someone opts in? After they book? After a no-show?
5. Which CRM / ESP / calendar? (GHL, ActiveCampaign, Kajabi, Calendly, ...)
6. Who writes the emails now?
7. What does a great student look like? (capital, job, timeline): this becomes the filter.

Disqualify if: no ads and no list, ticket under £2k, or they won't give you access to send from their domain.

## 6. Objections

- **"We already have someone on email."** → "Great. Is that person measured on booked and held calls, or on
  emails sent? The pilot runs on the list they aren't touching: people who never booked."
- **"Our show rate is fine."** → "What is it on cold traffic specifically? If it's above 75% you're ahead of
  most. Then the upside is the unbooked list, not the show rate."
- **"Send me a proposal."** → "Happy to. Can you share the size of the unbooked list and your close rate, so
  the numbers in it are yours and not guesses?"
