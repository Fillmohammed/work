# KittySpout: pitch pack

KittySpout is your first audit. The 32-page analysis you made is strong research, but a founder won't read
32 pages from a stranger. Turn it into **a 4-minute Loom + a 1-page summary + 3 finished concepts**. Offer the
full report as a follow-up ("I have the full 30-page breakdown if useful"). Promising more research is a good
reason for them to book the call.

---

## 1. The core insight

Your report's main finding, confirmed against their live ads in the Meta ad data (Parker, 9 Oct 2026):

- KittySpout has **~371 active ads**. All of the **20 longest-running** were launched 12 May – 9 June 2026.
  Of those 20:
  - **11** sell hydration / kidney / UTI health ("1 in 3 cats", "silently suffering", "dehydration").
  - **5** sell the offer (50% off + 5 free gifts).
  - **2** sell premium vs cheap ("buy once, cry once", the replacement cycle).
  - **2** are other (cats are wired for running water; feline acne).
  - **None** lead with the things owners actually write about when a fountain fails: **the cat refusing it,
    where the cat likes to drink, how hard it is to clean, and noise.**
- Their three AI animations (refill, washing, singing) are **one argument in three costumes**: same body, same
  three criteria, same CTA. That's hook testing, not angle testing.
- Their own Trustpilot shows the untapped angle:
  > "I would not have considered trying another one if not for the 365-day guarantee." (Edith, 5★)

  The buyer who already had a fountain fail, the "burned" buyer, needs a different argument than "bacteria
  bad". That's your pitch.

**One-line pitch:** *"You're running hundreds of ads that convince people a fountain is good for their cat.
You're not running ads for the people who already believe that, bought one, and watched their cat ignore it."*

## 2. Quick wins to flag (carefully, as help, not accusation)

From your report, section G1. These protect them from refunds, chargebacks and ad rejections:
1. **Offer mismatch:** the Notes static shows **60% OFF**; the primary text says **50% off**.
2. **Guarantee mismatch:** the ads say "365-day full money-back, send it back for a full refund". Their current
   refund page gives refunds for days 0–45, then exchange / store credit for days 46–365, with conditions.
   That gap creates angry customers.
3. **Disease claims:** "UTIs and kidney disease never get the chance to start" is an absolute medical outcome
   claim. Meta and the FTC scrutinise these. Hydration-support language is safer and still strong.
4. **Social proof varies:** 600,000 in the videos, 1 million in the primary text, 770,000 on the homepage.

In the email, mention these in **one line only**: "also spotted 2 offer mismatches worth fixing". Save the
details for the call. That gives them a reason to reply.

## 3. The 3 concepts (send these finished)

If you've already made ads for KittySpout, put your best ones into these slots instead of starting over.
Each concept should test a **new reason to buy**, not a new hook on the same reason.

### Concept A: "The third fountain" (cat acceptance) · UGC video, 45–60s
**For:** owners whose cat already ignored a fountain (Stage 4 "burned" buyer).
**Belief shift:** "my cat just doesn't do fountains" → "my cat didn't like *that* one, and I can let her decide
risk-free."

**Hooks (test 3):**
1. "My cat ignored two fountains. This is day 14 with the third."
2. "If your cat already rejected a fountain, watch this before you buy another one."
3. "I almost didn't buy a third cat fountain. Here's what changed my mind."

**Script:**

| Time | Visual | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Creator holding the old plastic fountain, the second one behind her in a cupboard | "My cat ignored two fountains. This is day 14 with the third." |
| 4–14s | Quick cuts: cat sniffing an old fountain and walking off; cat drinking from the bathroom tap | "The first one, she sniffed it once and walked away. The second, she used for a week, then went straight back to the bathroom tap. I'd decided she just wasn't a fountain cat." |
| 14–24s | KittySpout box arriving; creator reading the trial terms on her phone | "What got me to try again was that I could let *her* test it. [Exact current terms: e.g. 'If she doesn't use it, I can return it within 45 days.']" |
| 24–40s | Dated clips with on-screen "Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 6": cat staring, fountain moved next to the tap spot, first drink | "Day one, she just stared at it. Day three I moved it to where she already drinks. It's wireless, so I didn't need an outlet there. Day six…" *(real moment)* |
| 40–50s | Close-up of the faucet-style spout; cat drinking | "She's a tap cat, and the spout is basically a tap. And it's quiet enough that it doesn't spook her at night." |
| 50–60s | Creator to camera, fountain in frame | "If your cat's picky, don't take my word for it. Let your cat decide. Link's below." |

**Production notes:** needs a real owner, a real cat and dated footage (the setbacks are the proof; don't fake
day 1 success). Use the current return terms word for word. No disease claims.

**Primary text:**
> My cat ignored 2 fountains before this one. 🙃
> If yours has too, here's what I did differently: I let her test it, I put it where she already drinks
> (it's wireless), and I gave her a week.
> Day 6 she was drinking from it. Every cat's different, which is why the trial matters.
> 👉 Let your cat decide.

### Concept B: "Things I wish I knew before fountain #3" · Notes-style static
**Why this format:** their Notes-style static is a proven format for them. Keep the look, change its job from
"urgency" to "advice for burned buyers".

**On-image copy (Notes app, same visual style):**
> **Things I wish I knew before fountain #3 🐈**
> ✔️ My cat isn't "bad at fountains". She just liked the tap
> ✔️ Put it where SHE drinks, not where the outlet is
> ✔️ Give it a week (day 1 she stared, day 6 she drank)
> ✔️ Pick one you can fully take apart to clean
> ✔️ Check the return window BEFORE you buy

Product photo bottom-right; badge: "Let your cat test it" (no %, to avoid offer mismatches).

**Headline:** Picky cat? Let her decide.
**Primary text:** 3–4 lines matching the checklist, then the current offer and terms.

### Concept C: "She'll only drink here" (placement) · static or 20s B-roll
**For:** owners whose cat has one spot (bathroom, by the sink, a mug on the counter) with no outlet nearby.
**Belief shift:** "wireless is a feature" → "wireless means I can put water where my cat already drinks."

**Static, two panels:**
- Top: photo of a cat drinking from a mug by the bathroom cupboard. Text: *"She'll only drink HERE."*
  Small text: *"(there's no outlet here)"*
- Bottom: the same spot with the KittySpout. Text: *"So we brought the water to her."*

**Headline:** Put the water where your cat already drinks.
**Primary text:**
> Two fountains. Both by the outlet. She ignored both and kept drinking from a mug by the bathroom cupboard.
> KittySpout is wireless, so it went right where she drinks. [Battery/charging line, using the real spec.]

### Bonus test to suggest: the cleaning demo (video)
For owners who are tired of cleaning: show the **full, honestly timed** cleaning of the real product (pump,
filter, spout, basin).
Hooks: "I timed how long it takes to clean my cat's fountain." / "The part of your cat fountain you've never
cleaned." / "Why I gave away a $60 fountain: I needed a hammer to open it."
