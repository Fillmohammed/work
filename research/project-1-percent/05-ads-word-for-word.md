## How to use this file

Read each ad as the man in the persona would see it. Under each one there's a **Your notes** box. Useful questions:

1. Who is this ad talking to, and does that match the persona?
2. What does it promise, and does the page and checkout keep that promise?
3. Which numbers, names and prices does it use, and do they match the other ads?
4. Would he have a story to tell his partner after seeing it?
