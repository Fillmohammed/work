# Project 1%: consistency audit, from the ad to the finish

Checked on 10–11 Oct 2026 against:

- all 13 live ads (`05-ads-word-for-word.md`)
- the live pages and the full sales video (`06-funnel-pages-word-for-word.md`)

The test is Seth's (Module 5): **a brand is a promise; every touchpoint keeps it or breaks it.** Each item below says
what was promised, where, what actually happens, and how badly it hurts.

**Severity**

- 🔴 **Breaks trust or costs sales now.** Fix first.
- 🟠 **Confuses the story.** It's what makes this "a bunch, not a group".
- 🟡 **Polish.**

## What's consistent (keep it)

- **The story's core:** council estate, the property, the body breaking, the relationship ending, the floor of the
  apartment, the rebuild.
- **The format:** 35 lessons, one a day.
- **The 30-day money-back guarantee** (just fix how it fits the 35 days).
- **The source of the social proof:** 3,000+ clients and $10M+ in sales through Property Consulting, stated the same
  way everywhere.
- **"A private brotherhood" exists in every version.** It just needs to be real and the same size everywhere.
