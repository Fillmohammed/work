## 12. Lessons from the "6-Figure Creative Strategist" video, applied

- Use now:
  - Pick brands with a proven product (already spending on ads).
  - Borrow authority with competitor examples.
  - Use a narrow label.
  - Offer a "safer under Meta's health-ad rules" rewrite of a brand's 3 best ads as a paid foot in the door.
